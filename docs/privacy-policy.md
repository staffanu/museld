# museld privacy policy

museld is a laserdisc player: it decodes RF captures of laserdiscs that you have
made yourself and plays them from a file, a web server on your own network, or a
capture device on your own network.

museld collects no data.  It has no account, sends nothing to its author or to
anyone else, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.  What it
remembers between runs -- the addresses you opened and the settings you chose
for each disc -- stays on the device, in the app's own storage, and is used for
nothing but restoring those settings.

The Apple TV app asks for local network access because it reads captures from a
server or capture device on your network; the permission is used for that and
nothing else.  The sample capture included with the app is read from inside the
app.
